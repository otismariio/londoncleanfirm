Our Process

From your first enquiry to the final clean, we make the entire process straightforward in four steps

01

Tell us what you need

The customer contacts you, selects the cleaning service they need, and provides details about the property, preferred date, location, and any special requirements.

02

Get your quote

You assess the requirements, provide a clear quotation, agree on the scope of work, and confirm the cleaning date and time.

03

We get to work

Your cleaning team arrives as scheduled with the required equipment and carries out the agreed service with attention to detail and care.

04

Enjoy your clean space

The completed work is checked to ensure it meets your standards. The customer can review the service, raise any concerns, and the job is formally completed.
